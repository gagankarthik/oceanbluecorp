# Brand & content standard

The reference every line of copy on this site is written against. If a
sentence cannot be justified from this page, it does not ship.

---

## The Brand Triangle

### 1. Positioning: what we do

**Ocean Blue is the single accountable partner for the people, engineering,
platforms and operations an enterprise runs on.**

The category is not "IT staffing" and not "consulting". The real choice a buyer
is making is between:

> **one team that owns the outcome**, or **a stack of vendors pointing at each
> other when something breaks.**

That sentence is the whole position. Everything else is evidence for it.

Four practices, deliberately sold as one: **Talent · Engineering · Solutions ·
Managed Services**. We never describe them as a portfolio or a suite. They are
four faces of one team, under one contract, measured against one standard.

**Who we are for:** enterprises and state government agencies in North America.
Buyers with procurement, compliance and continuity obligations, who are
personally exposed when a vendor fails.

**What we are not:** a body shop, a marketplace, a reseller, a staffing agency
that disappears after placement.

### 2. Personality: how we say it

The voice of **a senior practitioner who has done the work**, not a
salesperson describing it. Specifically:

| We write | We don't |
|---|---|
| Short declaratives. One idea per sentence. | Stacked clauses that hedge. |
| Concrete nouns: "shortlists", "SLA", "engineers". | Abstractions: "solutions", "capabilities", "value". |
| Numbers when we have them. | Numbers we cannot evidence. |
| Plain verbs: build, run, hire, own, fix. | Inflated verbs: leverage, empower, unlock, elevate. |
| The reader as "you". | "Clients" and "organizations" in the third person. |
| Confidence through specifics. | Confidence through adjectives. |

**Banned vocabulary.** These words are the tell that nobody was actually
speaking: _leverage, synergy, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge,
seamless(ly), empower, unlock, robust, holistic, bespoke, journey,
game-changing, revolutionize, passionate, ecosystem, solutioning,
turnkey, state-of-the-art, next-generation, mission-critical_ (unless it
literally is), _thought leader, drive value, at scale_ (as a suffix).

**Also banned: the em dash as a decorative pause.** Use a comma, a full stop, or
rewrite. (Standing instruction from the client: it reads as machine-written.)

**Tone by surface:**

- **Hero / statements**: declarative, confident, no qualifiers.
- **Practice pages**: specific and technical. This reader knows the domain.
- **Careers**: direct and human. No "rockstars", no "family".
- **Errors and empty states**: say what happened and what to do. Never
  apologise twice, never blame the reader, never be cute.
- **Legal and policy**: plain English first, precision second. If a sentence
  needs a lawyer to parse, it needs rewriting, not a glossary.

### 3. Promise: what we deliver

**One team owns it end to end, and you have one number to call.**

The promise is only ever made in terms we can evidence:

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Shortlists in 48 hours | Operational commitment, stated as a standard |
| One contract, one SLA | Commercial model |
| Thirteen years | Founded 2013 |
| Certified MBE and WBE | NMSDC, Ohio MBE, Ohio WBE, City of Columbus MBE |
| Public sector ready | Existing state agency procurement history |

**Anything not in this table does not get asserted as fact.** Retention rates,
client counts and delivery-centre counts are CMS-editable and must be confirmed
by the client before they appear as claims.

## Rules that follow from the above

1. **Lead with the problem, not the company.** The reader does not care what we
   are until they know what we fix.
2. **Every page states one thing.** If a page argues three points, it persuades
   of none.
3. **A CTA names the next action, not the desire.** "Start a conversation",
   "See open roles". Never "Learn more", "Get started", "Discover".
4. **The same action keeps the same name everywhere.** The button that says
   "Start a conversation" leads to a page headed "Start a conversation".
5. **No borrowed copy, ever.** Every line on this site must be ours. (The
   careers CTA previously ran Accenture's wording; that is now replaced.)
6. **Empty states are invitations, not apologies.** Say what is coming and what
   to do meanwhile.

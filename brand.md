## Oceanblue in full

### Who we are

Oceanblue Solutions, Inc. is a technology services company founded on August 8, 2013 and headquartered in Powell, Ohio. In 2015 we signed our first Master Service Agreement with a prime vendor, and in 2021 we signed one with a Fortune 500 company. In 2022 we opened a delivery center in India, in 2024 we opened offices in the United Kingdom, and in 2025 we launched a dedicated practice for artificial intelligence. Today we serve enterprises and state government agencies across North America, with teams working from the United States, India and the United Kingdom.

We are a minority-owned and women-owned business, and we hold the certifications that prove it. That matters to the organizations we work with, because many of them, public agencies in particular, have supplier-diversity commitments they are required to meet and report on. When they work with us, they get a capable delivery partner and they meet those commitments at the same time.

### Our certifications

We hold four supplier-diversity certifications, each issued after an independent review of who owns and controls the company.

**NMSDC certified Minority Business Enterprise (MBE).** We are certified by the National Minority Supplier Development Council, the national body that certifies minority-owned businesses for corporate supplier-diversity programs. Large companies across the United States use NMSDC certification to find and count minority-owned suppliers, so this certification is what lets enterprise procurement teams include us in their diverse-spend programs.

**State of Ohio certified Minority Business Enterprise (MBE).** We are certified by the State of Ohio as a minority-owned business. State agencies in Ohio set aside contracts and spending goals for certified minority businesses, and this certification makes us eligible for that work and countable toward those goals.

**State of Ohio certified Women-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE).** We are also certified by the State of Ohio as a women-owned business. Agencies and companies with women-owned business goals can count work placed with us toward them.

**City of Columbus certified Minority Business Enterprise (MBE).** We are certified as a minority-owned business by the City of Columbus through its Office of Diversity and Inclusion. This makes us eligible for the City's minority business participation goals on city contracts in Columbus, our home region.

Together, these four certifications cover national corporate programs, state agency programs and city programs. A buyer at any of those levels can verify our status and buy through us with confidence.

### Our technology partnerships

Alongside our certifications, we hold partnerships with the platforms our clients run on.

**Amazon Web Services (AWS), Advanced Tier Services Partner.** AWS recognizes partners at tiers based on their certified staff, delivered projects and customer outcomes. Advanced Tier is the level AWS awards to partners with proven experience delivering AWS projects for customers. It means our cloud engineers work to AWS's own standards when they design, migrate and run workloads.

**Snowflake.** We work with Snowflake, the cloud data platform, to build data warehouses and analytics that teams can query and trust.

**Databricks.** We work with Databricks, the data and AI platform, to build data pipelines and take machine learning and AI models from pilot to production.

Because most of our cloud, data and AI delivery runs on these three platforms, clients get people who already know the stack they have, rather than people learning it on their budget.

### What we do

We do three things, and we do them as one team.

**We provide the people.** When a company needs skilled people faster than it can hire them, we supply them. Our IT staffing practice places vetted engineers, developers, analysts and architects who join a client's team and take ownership of the work. Clients can engage them on contract, hire them permanently, or have us run a whole team. We scope each role carefully, screen candidates technically, check backgrounds and references, and send a short, matched shortlist within 48 hours instead of a stack of résumés. We stay accountable for the people we place for the length of the engagement. Our engineering practice does the same for physical engineering: mechanical, electrical, civil, aerospace and controls engineers, on contract, as direct hires or as complete project teams. And because the best long-term answer is often to grow the team a client already has, our training practice runs instructor-led courses in cloud, DevOps, data and AI, security, ERP and Salesforce, including preparation for professional certifications.

**We build and modernize the platforms.** Our technology practices design, build and improve the systems a business runs on. Our cloud engineers move applications to AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud and modernize them once they are there, in planned, reversible steps so the business keeps running throughout, with security and cost control designed in from the start. Our cybersecurity team protects cloud environments, identity and applications, starting with the gaps an attacker would actually use and mapping every control to the frameworks the client must report against, so audits are evidence rather than scrambles. Our ERP team implements and integrates SAP, Oracle and Microsoft Dynamics to fit the way a business actually works. Our Salesforce team implements, develops, automates and administers Salesforce so it serves the people who use it every day. Our AI and data practice builds practical AI, automation, predictive analytics and the data engineering underneath them, secure and tied to real business decisions. And our digital transformation practice sets the technology strategy, the architecture and a roadmap with outcomes that can be measured.

**We keep it running.** Once systems are live, someone has to look after them. Our managed services team monitors infrastructure and applications around the clock, runs the helpdesk, applies patches and manages the infrastructure, all to one service level agreement. The client has one team responsible and one number to call.

We also build and run our own software, with the same people and the same standards as our client work. Blue-IQ reads documents of every kind, from PDFs and Word files to scans and phone photos, and turns them into structured data, returning a confidence score for every field and flagging anything it is unsure of so a person only reviews what needs judgement. Inytes is our digital invitation product. Running our own products in production keeps us honest about what it takes to own software after it ships.

Every engagement, whatever the practice, follows the same four steps: we learn the business and the outcome that matters, we design the solution and the roadmap with the client and agree how success will be measured, we deliver in short increments, and then we keep improving against the agreed service levels with regular reviews. Clients can work with us as contract specialists, as direct hires, as a project team that owns a defined outcome, or as a managed service where we run the work for them.

The industries we know best are government and the public sector, healthcare, financial services and manufacturing: places where delivery, compliance and uptime all matter at once, and where a vendor that disappears after the contract is signed is not an option.

## How we sound

The voice of a senior practitioner who has done the work, not a salesperson describing it.

- Short, plain sentences. One idea each.
- Concrete nouns ("shortlist", "SLA", "engineers") over abstractions ("solutions", "value").
- Plain verbs: build, run, hire, own, fix.
- Speak to the reader as "you".
- Numbers only when we can prove them.
- No buzzwords (leverage, synergy, world-class, seamless, empower, unlock and the rest), and no decorative em dashes.
